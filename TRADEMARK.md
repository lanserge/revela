# Trademark

"revela" is the name of this project. The licence covers the code, not the name —
see Section 6 of the licence. Forks and derivatives should choose a different
name; you may state compatibility ("based on revela") but not present the name as
your own.

## Where that comes from

revela is released under the Solderpad Hardware License v2.1, which is a
wraparound over the Apache License, Version 2.0 and incorporates its terms. That
includes **Section 6, "Trademarks"**, which states that the licence

> does not grant permission to use the trade names, trademarks, service marks, or
> product names of the Licensor, except as required for reasonable and customary
> use in describing the origin of the Work and reproducing the content of the
> NOTICE file.

So this is not an extra restriction layered on top of an open licence. It is what
the licence you already have says. This file exists to make it easy to find, and
to say what "reasonable and customary use in describing the origin" looks like in
practice.

## What is fine, without asking

- Saying your work is **based on**, **derived from**, **built with**, **compatible
  with** or **a fork of** revela.
- Naming revela in documentation, papers, talks, comparisons, blog posts and
  commit messages.
- Reproducing the [NOTICE](NOTICE) file and the SPDX headers, which name the
  project — that is required by the licence, not merely permitted.
- Keeping the name in unmodified files you redistribute.

## What to avoid

- Naming your fork, product, company or service "revela", or a name close enough
  to be confused with it.
- Presenting a modified version as though it were this project — the concern is
  that somebody reading your name cannot tell whose work they are getting, and
  therefore who is answerable for it.
- Using the name in a way that implies endorsement, affiliation or certification
  by this project or its maintainer.

If you are unsure, ask: **s.rabykin@gmail.com**. Permission for a specific use is
usually straightforward to give in writing.

## House style for the mark

The name is written **revela™**, and only that way. No other trademark marking
appears anywhere in this repository or in anything it generates, and nothing in
either may describe the mark as registered. If you are editing this repository,
keep it that way.
